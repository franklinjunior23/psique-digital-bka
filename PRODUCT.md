## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (user's choice). One page only: the home page (`/`). No other pages are created; the current site's "Documentación" page is out of scope.

## Users

- **Primary audience:** young people and adults (Spanish-speaking, contact number in Peru, +51) who want to understand their emotional wellbeing and their emotions, and to receive psychological support from home. They often arrive unsure or anxious and are deciding whether to reach out.
- **Owners/operators:** the Psique Digital BKA team (the user selected "psychologists/clinicians" as the main user). The site represents their practice to the audience above.

## Product Purpose

Psique Digital BKA is a digital initiative for information, guidance and support in psychological wellbeing. It aims to bring psychology into people's everyday lives. The site presents the initiative and moves the visitor to start a conversation. Success means the visitor reaches out through WhatsApp (the "Chat en directo" button) or follows the social channels.

The current work is a **restructure and visual upgrade of the existing Google Sites home page**, keeping its content.

## Positioning

Virtual psychological care that is accessible, confidential and personalised, using digital tools to make access easier. It combines psychology and technology, with a close, empathetic, inclusive approach that respects each person's own process. Tagline already in use: "La psicología que suena contigo".

## Product Principles

1. **Reaching out should feel safe and easy.** WhatsApp contact is always one obvious step away.
2. **Clarity over decoration.** Content the visitor needs (what we help with, for whom, how) is visible, not buried in collapsibles.
3. **Honest care.** No invented credentials, statistics or testimonials. Confidentiality and respect are stated plainly.
4. **Close, not clinical.** The tone is human and everyday, matching "la psicología que suena contigo".

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The audience may be anxious or distressed, so the page needs calm, legible reading, sufficient contrast, and plain language. Mobile-first: most visitors arrive from WhatsApp, TikTok and Instagram on their phones. No specific WCAG level has been mandated; WCAG 2.2 AA is the working floor.
